I designed the Campus Customs dashboard to help the shop owner manage customer tickets and see what the AI agents are doing. I wanted to keep the design simple so that the owner can easily find the information they need.

I used a navy header, a light background, and orange buttons to make the dashboard easy to navigate and the main action easy to find. I divided the dashboard into two sections. The left side shows the three open tickets, with the ticket number, customer name, and a short description. The right side shows the selected ticket and the option to start the agent team. This makes it easier to focus on one ticket at a time while still seeing the other requests.

I also added an activity feed where the owner can follow the agents' work and see which tools they use. The checking balance is displayed at the top because cash is important when making business decisions. Ticket status labels help the owner track which tickets are open or resolved.

I made the layout responsive so that the ticket list and workspace appear side by side on larger screens and stack vertically on smaller screens.

Overall, I wanted to make the dashboard easy to use and give the shop owner more visibility into the agents' work instead of showing only the final result. The live agent workflow still needs to be tested to confirm that it works as expected.
